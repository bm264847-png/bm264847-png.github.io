05

Good to know before you apply

  1. 01

    English or Turkish

    Many programs are taught fully in English. Turkish-taught programs usually include a one-year language preparation year.

  2. 02

    Programme Length

    Bachelor degrees normally take 4 years; law and medicine take 5–6 years. Master and PhD options are also available.

  3. 03

    Application Season

    Most intakes open in spring for the autumn semester. Early applications give the best chance of a place and a scholarship.

  4. 04

    Scholarships

    Many universities offer partial or full tuition scholarships based on academic merit. We advise you on where your profile fits best.

    Applying through Turkey Trainings does not guarantee admission — you still need to meet the academic entry requirements of your chosen university. What we do is make sure your application is complete, well-presented and sent to the universities where you have the strongest chance.
