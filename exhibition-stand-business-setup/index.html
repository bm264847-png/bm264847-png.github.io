02 — Specialized Industry Program

We don't sell a classroom course. We transfer the operating know-how behind a real exhibition stand business.

This international professional program combines Kongre Stand Kirala's 19 years of real exhibition-industry experience with Turkey Trainings' tailor-made training and international program infrastructure. From workshop setup and production systems to pricing, sales, installation and client acquisition — we help serious companies, investors and entrepreneurs enter or scale the exhibition stand sector with less trial-and-error.

The knowledge areas covered in the professional engagement. Proprietary methods, supplier data, machinery lists and pricing formulas are shared inside the paid program — not published publicly.

    Business Model & Market Entry

    Workshop Planning

    Equipment Strategy

  7. 07

    From client brief to concept, technical preparation, production and installation.

  8. 08

    Sales & Client Acquisition

    How to identify events, exhibitors and high-probability corporate stand buyers.

08

Frequently asked questions

Can you help us start an exhibition stand business in our country?

Yes. The program can be tailored for new investors or existing companies entering the exhibition sector. Scope depends on your country, current infrastructure, team and objectives.

Do we need an existing workshop or factory?

No. The program can start from business and workshop planning, or help an existing printing, signage, LED, events, interiors or fabrication company add an exhibition stand division.

Is this a standard course?

No. It is a professional business-setup, training and know-how-transfer engagement designed around your situation.

Can the program be delivered outside Türkiye?

Yes. Depending on scope, delivery may be in Türkiye, in your country, or hybrid.

Can you train our existing team?

Yes. Tailor-made training can be designed for management, production, sales, costing, installation and project teams.

How much does the program cost?

Every engagement is individually designed according to your country, existing infrastructure, team and required level of know-how transfer. Fees are quoted only after a confidential initial assessment.

How do we start?

Request a confidential assessment through our contact page, or message the project directly on WhatsApp at +90 551 233 23 66.
