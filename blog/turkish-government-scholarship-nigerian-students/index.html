Turkish Government Scholarship for Nigerian Students: The Complete 2026 Guide

Scroll

01

What Is the Türkiye Bursları (Türkiye Scholarships) Programme?

Türkiye Bursları is the flagship scholarship programme managed by the Republic of Türkiye's Presidency for Turks Abroad and Related Communities (YTB). Every year, the Turkish government invites thousands of international students — including hundreds from Nigeria and across Africa — to pursue undergraduate, master's, doctoral, and research programmes at top Turkish universities.

This is not a partial scholarship. It is a fully-funded, government-backed award that covers every major expense of your academic life in Türkiye. For Nigerian students who qualify, it represents one of the most transformative opportunities available anywhere in the world.

If you are serious about studying in Türkiye, explore your options on our Study in Türkiye page before you apply — understanding which programmes fit your goals is the first step.

02

What Does the Turkish Government Scholarship Cover?

The scholarship package for international students typically includes:

  • Monthly stipend throughout your studies
  • University tuition — paid directly to the university
  • Accommodation in government-approved student dormitories
  • Health insurance valid across Türkiye
  • One return airfare between Nigeria and Türkiye (at the start and end of your programme)
  • Turkish language course — a free one-year intensive programme before you begin your degree

This package means you arrive, you study, and you graduate — without carrying a student debt burden. That is an exceptional offer compared to most Western scholarship programmes, which cover only partial costs or require repayment.

03

Who Is Eligible? Key Requirements for Nigerian Applicants

The Turkish government scholarship for Nigerian students follows a set of eligibility criteria that are more accessible than most people expect:

Academic requirements:

  • Undergraduate: minimum cumulative GPA of 70% or equivalent (WAEC/NECO grades considered)
  • Master's: minimum bachelor's GPA of 75%
  • Doctoral: minimum master's GPA of 75%

Age limits:

  • Undergraduate applicants: under 21 years old at the time of application
  • Master's applicants: under 30 years old
  • Doctoral applicants: under 35 years old

Nationality: Nigerian citizens are fully eligible. Students who are already enrolled in a Turkish university programme are not eligible to apply.

Language: English-taught programmes are widely available, and the one-year Turkish language course prepares you for Turkish-taught options. Many Nigerian students succeed in both formats.

The scholarship is highly competitive. Strong academic results, clear motivation, and a well-matched programme choice are what separate successful applicants.

04

How to Apply: Step-by-Step Process

Step 1 — Open the Official Application Portal

Applications are submitted exclusively through the Türkiye Bursları online portal (turkiyeburslari.gov.tr). The application window typically opens in January and closes in February each year. Mark your calendar — late applications are not accepted.

Step 2 — Choose Your Programme and University

Türkiye has over 200 universities accepting scholarship students. Before applying, research programmes across disciplines: medicine, engineering, business administration, agriculture, international relations, education, and more. Browse universities accepting international students here to shortlist institutions that match your ambitions.

You may select up to twelve programmes in order of preference during your application. Prioritise programmes where your academic background is strongest.

Step 3 — Prepare Your Documents

You will need:

  • Your academic transcripts (O-level and A-level / post-secondary results, certified)
  • A valid international passport (at least 18 months remaining validity)
  • A personal statement / motivation letter
  • Letters of recommendation (typically two)
  • Proof of language proficiency (where required)
  • A recent passport photograph

All documents must be translated into English or Turkish if they are in another language. Notaried copies may be required for final submission.

Step 4 — Submit Your Online Application

Fill in all sections of the application portal carefully. Double-check every academic detail — errors or inconsistencies are common reasons for rejection. Upload your documents in the specified formats and submit before the deadline.

Step 5 — Interview and Placement Decision

Shortlisted candidates are invited to an online interview conducted in English. The interview panel assesses your academic background, motivation, and fit. Following the interview, successful candidates receive a placement letter indicating which university and programme they have been awarded.

05

Life as a Nigerian Student in Türkiye

Türkiye is home to a growing and vibrant African student community, with thousands of Nigerian students already studying in cities like Istanbul, Ankara, Konya, Trabzon, and Bursa. You will find:

  • Active Nigerian student associations at most major universities
  • Halal food widely available across every city
  • A welcoming, multicultural campus environment
  • Strong English-speaking networks in international student communities
  • Modern, well-equipped campuses with digital libraries and research facilities

Turkish cities are also remarkably affordable by international standards. Your monthly stipend typically covers day-to-day living with room to explore, travel, and build your professional network.

06

Tips to Strengthen Your Application

Nigerian students who win the Turkish government scholarship typically do the following:

  • Apply early — do not wait until the final week; system congestion causes last-minute problems.
  • Write a compelling motivation letter — generic letters fail. Explain specifically why you chose Türkiye, why this programme, and what you plan to do with your degree.
  • Select realistic programmes — applying to twelve top-ranked programmes all in one city may narrow your chances. Diversify your selections geographically.
  • Get your transcripts certified in advance — processing time at NYSC, WAEC, or your university can take weeks.
  • Practise your interview — typical questions cover your study plans, your field of interest, and your goals after graduation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Nigerian students apply for the Turkish government scholarship even without an IELTS certificate? Yes. Many Turkish universities — particularly those offering programmes taught in Turkish — do not require IELTS for admission. For English-taught programmes, proof of English proficiency (including school records from an English-medium institution) is often accepted in place of a standardised test score.

Is it possible to work part-time while on the Turkish government scholarship? International students in Türkiye may work part-time under certain conditions once they have completed their first year of study and obtained a work permit. However, the scholarship stipend is designed to cover your living costs — most scholarship holders focus on their academic work.

Does the scholarship cover postgraduate (master's and PhD) programmes? Yes. The Türkiye Bursları programme offers separate scholarship tracks for undergraduate, master's, and doctoral students. Each track has its own eligibility criteria, with the master's and doctoral scholarships also including a research support component.

What happens if I fail a semester? Scholarship recipients are expected to maintain satisfactory academic progress. Repeated failure to meet university requirements can result in suspension or termination of the scholarship. Students facing difficulties should seek support from their academic advisors early.

How competitive is the Turkish government scholarship for Nigerian students? The scholarship receives over 150,000 applications globally each year for a limited number of placements. Nigerian applicants compete within a regional Africa pool. Strong academic records, a compelling motivation letter, and a well-researched programme selection are the key differentiators.
