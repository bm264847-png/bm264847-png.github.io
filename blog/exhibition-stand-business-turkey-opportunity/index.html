Exhibition Stand Business in Türkiye: A Lucrative Opportunity for African Entrepreneurs

Scroll

01

Why Exhibition Stands Are Big Business in Türkiye

Türkiye ranks among the top 10 countries globally for hosting trade fairs and exhibitions. With world-class venues in Istanbul, Ankara, Antalya and beyond, the country welcomes over 500 international trade events every year — from agriculture and healthcare to defence and energy.

Every exhibitor at these events needs a professional, eye-catching stand. This has created a thriving ecosystem of stand design, production and installation companies. For entrepreneurs who understand the craft, the margins are attractive and repeat business is almost guaranteed.

02

The Opportunity for African Entrepreneurs

Many African countries are rapidly expanding their own exhibition and events industries. Lagos, Accra, Nairobi and Abuja now host dozens of international expos annually, yet professional stand companies remain scarce.

This gap represents a massive opportunity:

  • Import the know-how from Türkiye's mature exhibition industry to your home market.
  • Offer premium services that exhibitors currently struggle to find locally.
  • Build a cross-border operation serving fairs in both Africa and Türkiye.

03

What Does the Business Involve?

An exhibition stand company typically handles:

  • Design — Creating 3D visualizations and floor plans tailored to each client's brand and booth size.
  • Production — Manufacturing modular stand structures, graphics, lighting and furniture.
  • Logistics — Transporting, assembling and dismantling stands at venue sites.
  • On-site management — Ensuring everything runs smoothly during the event.

The beauty of this model is that it scales: you can start with basic shell-scheme enhancements and grow into fully custom builds.

04

How Turkey Trainings Can Help

Turkey Trainings has partnered with Kongre Stand Kirala, one of Türkiye's established exhibition-services companies, to offer a structured Exhibition Stand Business Setup & Know-How Transfer programme.

This programme covers:

  • Real-world observation at active trade fairs in Türkiye.
  • Hands-on workshops in stand design, production and project management.
  • Business-plan development and market-entry strategy for your target country.
  • Ongoing mentorship from experienced professionals.

05

Who Should Consider This Programme?

  • Aspiring entrepreneurs looking for a profitable, skills-based business.
  • Event-management companies that want to add stand services to their portfolio.
  • Investors exploring the events and MICE sector.
  • Professionals already in construction, interior design or branding who want to diversify.

06
