01

Why Choose Türkiye for Professional Training?

  • Practical, hands-on learning — programmes combine classroom sessions with real site visits and demonstrations.
  • Sector expertise — Türkiye is strong in agriculture, healthcare, education, tourism, technology and public administration.
  • Excellent value — high-quality training and hospitality at competitive costs.
  • A gateway experience — participants gain professional knowledge while experiencing Türkiye’s culture and building networks.

02

Two Ways to Train: Open vs In-House

Open Training Programmes

Open programmes run on scheduled dates and are ideal for individuals or small groups. Participants from different organisations learn together, share experiences and network. This is a flexible way to upskill a few staff members without organising a full private programme.

In-House (Custom) Training Programmes

In-house programmes are designed exclusively for a single institution. The content, level, duration, site visits and certification are all tailored to your organisation’s goals. This is the preferred choice for ministries, agencies and companies that want to train a whole team around specific objectives — delivered as a private group programme in Türkiye or online.

03

Popular Training Sectors

Institutions from across Africa train in areas such as:

  • Leadership and management — building stronger public and private-sector leaders.
  • Agriculture and agribusiness — modern farming, aquaculture, poultry and food systems.
  • Healthcare and public health — clinical practice, administration and health systems.
  • Education and teacher development — modern pedagogy and school leadership.
  • Tourism and hospitality — service excellence and destination management.
  • Technology and digital transformation — practical, future-focused skills.

04

How a Group Training Programme Is Organised

  • Define your goals. Identify the skills your team needs and the outcomes you want.
  • Design the programme. Content, duration, level and site visits are agreed together.
  • Handle the logistics. Invitation letters, scheduling, venues, accommodation and transfers are arranged.
  • Deliver the training. Expert-led sessions combine theory with practical, real-world visits.
  • Certify and follow up. Participants receive certificates, and outcomes can be reviewed for future cooperation.

05

Certificates and Practical Learning

Participants typically receive a certificate of completion, and strong programmes go beyond the classroom — with site visits, demonstrations and hands-on sessions that make the learning immediately useful back home.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between open and in-house training?

Open training programmes have scheduled dates that individuals and small groups can join. In-house (custom) training is designed exclusively for one organisation, tailored to its goals, and can be delivered as a private group programme in Türkiye or online.

Can training be tailored to our institution’s specific needs?

Yes. In-house programmes are built around your objectives — content, level, duration, site visits and certification can all be customised for your team.

Do participants receive a certificate?

Yes. Participants typically receive a certificate of completion, and programmes can include practical site visits and hands-on sessions alongside classroom learning.
