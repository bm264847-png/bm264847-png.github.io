Healthcare & Medical Training in Türkiye for African Professionals

Scroll

01

Türkiye's Healthcare Transformation

Over the past 15 years, Türkiye has undergone one of the most impressive healthcare transformations in the world. The country now has:

  • Over 1,500 hospitals, including internationally accredited facilities.
  • A thriving medical tourism industry serving over 1.2 million international patients annually.
  • Advanced capabilities in organ transplantation, oncology, cardiology and cosmetic surgery.
  • A rapidly growing health technology sector.

This transformation has created deep expertise that is now being shared with international partners — and African healthcare professionals are among the biggest beneficiaries.

02

Training Programmes Available

Turkey Trainings organises certified medical and healthcare training programmes tailored to the needs of African health systems:

Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) Training

Comprehensive pre-hospital emergency care: patient assessment, airway management, cardiac emergencies, trauma care and ambulance operations. Includes practical sessions in simulation centres and hospital emergency departments.

Nursing Assistant Certificate Programme

Designed for those entering the nursing profession: patient care fundamentals, vital signs monitoring, infection control, medication administration basics and clinical practice in Turkish hospitals.

Midwifery Training

Antenatal and postnatal care, safe delivery techniques, newborn assessment, family planning and managing obstetric emergencies. Delivered in collaboration with Turkish university hospitals.

Hospital Management & Administration

For health administrators and policy-makers: hospital operations, quality management (JCI standards), health information systems, budgeting and patient-flow optimisation.

Medical Certificate Programmes

Specialised short courses in areas such as radiology technology, laboratory techniques, physiotherapy basics and public health.

03

Why Train in Türkiye?

World-Class Facilities

Participants train in modern hospitals equipped with the latest technology — the same facilities that serve international patients from across the globe.

Experienced Instructors

Programmes are delivered by practicing physicians, senior nurses, university faculty and hospital managers with decades of experience.

Affordable and Accessible

Compared to medical training in Europe or North America, programmes in Türkiye offer exceptional value without compromising on quality.

Practical Focus

Every programme includes substantial hands-on clinical or simulation components — not just classroom lectures.

International Certification

All participants receive completion certificates that are recognised and valued by employers and institutions.

04

Who Should Apply?

  • Nurses and midwives seeking advanced skills and international exposure.
  • Paramedics and EMTs looking to upgrade their emergency-care capabilities.
  • Health ministry officials exploring capacity-building for their workforce.
  • Hospital administrators wanting to benchmark against international standards.
  • Medical graduates seeking practical clinical experience.

05

How to Get Started

Turkey Trainings works with both individual professionals and institutions (ministries, hospitals, NGOs) to design programmes that match specific needs and schedules.

Programmes can be arranged as:

  • Scheduled open trainings — join an upcoming cohort.
  • Custom group programmes — designed exclusively for your team (minimum 5 participants).

Training duration typically ranges from 2 weeks to 3 months depending on the programme and depth of specialisation required.
