Türkiye Student Visa Guide for African Students (2026)

Scroll

01

Do You Need a Student Visa?

Yes. If you plan to study in Türkiye, you must enter the country on a student visa obtained before you travel. It is not possible to arrive on a tourist visa and switch to student status. The student visa is the legal gateway that later allows you to obtain your residence permit.

02

Step 1: Get Your Admission Letter First

The visa process cannot begin without an official letter of acceptance from a Turkish university. This confirms you have been admitted to a recognised programme and is the cornerstone document for your application.

03

Step 2: Book an Appointment at the Turkish Embassy or Consulate

Apply through the Turkish embassy or consulate responsible for your country or region. Visit their official channel, complete the student visa application form, and book an appointment. Do this early — appointment slots can fill up during peak intake months.

04

Step 3: Prepare Your Visa Documents

While requirements can vary slightly by country, you should generally prepare:

  • A valid passport (with sufficient remaining validity).
  • Your university admission/acceptance letter.
  • A completed student visa application form.
  • Passport-sized photographs.
  • Proof of financial means to support your studies and living costs.
  • Proof of accommodation in Türkiye where requested.
  • Payment of the visa fee.
Always confirm the exact list with your local Turkish consulate, as some add country-specific requirements.

05

Step 4: Attend Your Appointment and Submit

Attend your appointment in person, submit your documents and biometrics if required, and pay the fee. Answer any questions honestly and clearly. Keep copies of everything you submit.

06

Step 5: Wait for Processing

Processing times differ by consulate and season but often take a few weeks. This is exactly why you should start as soon as your admission letter arrives — you want your visa comfortably before orientation.

07

Step 6: Travel to Türkiye

Once your visa is approved, you can book your flights and travel. Carry your admission letter, passport, visa and supporting documents in your hand luggage for immigration on arrival.

08

Step 7: Apply for Your Residence Permit

This step is essential and often overlooked. After arriving, you must apply for a student residence permit within the required period. Your university’s international office typically guides students through this, and it is what allows you to stay legally for the length of your studies.

09

Tips for a Smooth Visa Experience

  • Start early. The admission letter, appointment and processing all take time.
  • Be thorough. Missing or inconsistent documents are the top reason for delays.
  • Keep copies. Digital and printed copies of every document help at each stage.
  • Follow official sources. Rely on the Turkish consulate’s official guidance for your country.

Frequently asked questions

Can I travel to Türkiye on a tourist visa and then study?

No. To study you must enter Türkiye on a student visa obtained from a Turkish embassy or consulate before you travel, using your university admission letter. You cannot convert a tourist entry into a study status.

How long does the student visa take to process?

Processing times vary by country and consulate but often take a few weeks. Apply as soon as you receive your admission letter to leave enough time before classes begin.

What is the difference between the student visa and the residence permit?

The student visa lets you enter Türkiye to study. After you arrive, you apply for a student residence permit, which is what allows you to stay legally for the duration of your studies.
