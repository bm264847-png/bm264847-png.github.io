Best Universities in Türkiye for International Students (2026 Guide)

Scroll

01

What Makes a University a Great Fit?

The right university is about more than rankings. Weigh these factors together:

  • Your programme. Does the university offer your subject at the level and quality you want?
  • Language of instruction. Do you want an English-taught or Turkish-taught programme?
  • Cost. Consider tuition alongside the living costs of the city.
  • Scholarships. Which universities offer awards or discounts you qualify for?
  • Location. Big city energy (like Istanbul or Ankara) or a calmer, more affordable city?
  • Recognition. Is the degree respected in your home country and internationally?

02

Public vs Private (Foundation) Universities

Public universities are often more affordable and include many of Türkiye’s oldest and most respected institutions. Private (foundation) universities typically offer more English-taught programmes, modern facilities, smaller classes and generous scholarship discounts. Neither is universally “better” — the right one depends on your goals and budget.

03

English-Taught Programmes

A key advantage for international students is the wide availability of English-taught programmes, especially at private universities. This means you can earn a full, recognised degree without first learning Turkish — though picking up the language while you are there is a wonderful bonus.

04

Popular Student Cities

  • Istanbul — Türkiye’s largest, most vibrant city, with the widest range of universities.
  • Ankara — the capital, home to several leading institutions and a strong student scene.
  • Izmir — a relaxed coastal city with a great quality of life.
  • Smaller cities — places like Konya, Kayseri and Eskişehir offer excellent value and a focused student experience.

05

Are Turkish Degrees Recognised?

Yes. Turkish universities feature in international rankings and their diplomas are widely recognised across Africa, Europe and the rest of the world — making a Turkish degree a solid foundation for your career wherever you go next.

06

How to Make Your Shortlist

  • List your must-haves — programme, language and budget.
  • Match universities that meet those criteria.
  • Compare scholarships, city and facilities.
  • Check entry requirements so you apply where you are competitive.
  • Apply to several to maximise your chances.

Frequently asked questions

Are Turkish university degrees recognised internationally?

Yes. Turkish universities appear in international rankings and their degrees are widely recognised around the world, including across Africa, Europe and beyond.

Which is better — a public or a private university in Türkiye?

Both can be excellent. Public universities are often more affordable, while private (foundation) universities frequently offer more English-taught programmes, modern facilities and scholarships. The best choice depends on your budget, programme and language preference.

How do I know which university is right for me?

Look at your programme, budget, language of instruction, city and scholarship options together. Turkey Trainings helps match your profile to universities where you are most likely to be admitted and to thrive.
