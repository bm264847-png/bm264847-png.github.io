01 — Aviation training in Europe

Professional Aviation Training in Türkiye & Italy

Turkey Trainings provides professional aviation and aerospace training programmes in Türkiye and Italy for pilots, airlines, airports, maintenance organisations, aviation professionals, government institutions and international delegations.

Programmes are delivered through our specialised aviation training network and are tailored to the participant’s background, the organisation’s objectives and the practical and regulatory requirements of each training.

Our portfolio ranges from pilot licence and flight training to airport operations, airline operations, aircraft maintenance, aviation safety, compliance, quality management and EASA-oriented professional training.

We work with individual participants, corporate teams, airlines, airport operators, aviation authorities, maintenance organisations, ministries and sponsored delegations — including many institutions from Africa and the wider international community seeking training in Europe.

Training schedules, entry requirements and certification vary by programme. Every request is assessed individually, and Turkey Trainings issues a complete, tailor-made proposal.

Training Locations

Türkiye & Italy

Programmes are available in Türkiye and Italy depending on the selected training, practical requirements, participant profile, regulatory conditions and provider availability.

  • 32 programmes across four aviation training groups
  • Individual, corporate and institutional participation
  • Flight and practical training in person only; selected theory online where confirmed
  • No fixed public prices — Contact Turkey Trainings for dates, availability and a customised quotation.

03 — Groups A & B · Pilot Licence, Flight & Advanced Pilot Training

Pilot & Flight Training

From a first recreational licence to structured hour building, night flying and aircraft differences, our pilot programmes combine ground theory with practical flight instruction. Flight components are always delivered in person, in Türkiye or Italy depending on the programme.

  • Private Pilot Licence – PPL Training
  • Light Aircraft Pilot Licence – LAPL Training
  • Pilot Hour Building & Advanced Flight Training
  • Aviation English & Radiotelephony Training

04 — Group C · Airline, Airport & Ground Operations

Airline & Airport Operations Training

Operational training for dispatchers, operations control staff, cabin crew, ramp and passenger-service teams and airport managers — the people who keep the operation safe, punctual and customer-focused.

  • Flight Dispatcher & Airline OCC Training
  • Cabin Crew Professional Training
  • Airport Airside & Ground Operations Training

05 — Groups C & D · Maintenance, Planning & Technical Skills

Aircraft Maintenance & Technical Training

Maintenance planning, human factors, wiring awareness, fuel tank safety and technical English for technicians, planners and engineering teams working in regulated maintenance environments.

  • Aircraft Maintenance Planning – Part 145 / CAMO / CAO
  • Human Factors in Aircraft Maintenance Training
  • EWIS – Electrical Wiring Interconnection System Training

Safety management systems, risk assessment, crew resource management, dangerous goods, FOD prevention, auditing and aerospace quality management for organisations that take safety culture seriously.
