02 — Programme Overview

About this programme

Many pilots need additional flight time and broader experience before moving on to their next qualification. A structured hour-building programme turns those hours into genuine skill development rather than simply time in the logbook.

Alongside hour building, Turkey Trainings can arrange Night Rating flight training, Aircraft Differences modules and Garmin GTN650 navigation and radio systems training in Italy, subject to confirmation.

Training location for this programme: Italy. Dates and final arrangements are confirmed following assessment of the participant or institutional request.

03

Learning objectives

  • Build flight time efficiently with clear, progressive objectives
  • Strengthen navigation, planning and decision-making in varied conditions
  • Extend privileges to night flying where entry requirements are met
  • Gain familiarity with aircraft differences such as VP, RU, EFIS, SLPC and turbocharged engines
  • Use modern navigation and communication systems with confidence

05 — Who Should Attend

Target participants

  • Licensed pilots building hours towards further qualifications
  • Pilots meeting the entry requirements for night rating training
  • Trainee and licensed pilots preparing for professional flying pathways

06 — Curriculum

Programme content

  • 1 · Pilot Hour Building Programme — planned cross-country and local flying
  • 2 · Night Rating Flight Training — theory and dual night flying (subject to confirmation)
  • 3 · Aircraft Differences Training — VP, RU, EFIS, SLPC, Turbocharged and SEP/MEP differences (each module subject to confirmation)
  • 4 · Garmin GTN650 Navigation & Radio Systems Training (subject to confirmation)
  • 5 · Flight planning, weather assessment and airmanship reviews

07 — Programme Details

Format, requirements & certification

  1. 01

    Training format

    Theory combined with practical flight training. Flight components are always delivered in person and are never offered online.

  2. 02

    Training location

    Training location for this programme: Italy. Dates and final arrangements are confirmed following assessment of the participant or institutional request.

  3. 03

    Duration & dates

    Available on Request. Contact Turkey Trainings for dates, availability and a customised quotation.

  4. 04

    Entry requirements

    A valid pilot licence and medical certificate are normally required. Specific requirements for night rating and differences training depend on the module and applicable regulations, and are confirmed during assessment.

  5. 05

    Certification

    Certification details are confirmed according to the selected programme and training provider.

  6. 06

    Customisation

    Packages can combine hour building with night rating, differences modules and systems training into a single plan, scheduled around the participant’s availability.
