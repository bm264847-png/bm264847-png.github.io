02 — Programme Overview

About this programme

Misunderstanding on the radio remains a known contributor to aviation incidents. Aviation English training strengthens standard phraseology and the plain language needed when situations fall outside routine.

Turkey Trainings offers Aviation English Radiotelephony Training, Aviation English Language Proficiency Preparation (subject to confirmation) and Technical English for Aviation. Radiotelephony and Technical English are delivered in Italy or, for theory-based sessions, online where confirmed; the training location for Language Proficiency Preparation is confirmed according to programme and provider availability.

Training location by module — Aviation English Radiotelephony Training, Technical English for Aviation: Italy; Aviation English Language Proficiency Preparation: training location confirmed according to programme and provider availability.

03

Learning objectives

  • Use standard radiotelephony phraseology accurately and confidently
  • Communicate clearly in non-routine and emergency situations
  • Expand operational and technical aviation vocabulary
  • Improve listening comprehension of varied accents and radio quality
  • Prepare for aviation English language proficiency assessment

05 — Who Should Attend

Target participants

  • Student and licensed pilots
  • Airline, airport and air navigation personnel
  • Maintenance staff working with English-language documentation
  • Aviation authority staff and sponsored delegations

06 — Curriculum

Programme content

  • 1 · International radiotelephony procedures and standard phraseology
  • 2 · Plain English for non-routine and emergency communication
  • 3 · Aviation terminology for operations, weather and navigation
  • 4 · Listening practice with realistic radio exchanges
  • 5 · Language proficiency preparation: fluency, structure, interaction (subject to confirmation)
  • 6 · Technical English for manuals, task cards and technical reports

08 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can Aviation English be taken online?

Yes, theory-based Aviation English sessions can be delivered online where the provider confirms it. Classroom delivery takes place in Italy.

Does this course include the official proficiency test?

The preparation course focuses on developing the required skills. Whether an assessment can be arranged depends on the selected programme and is confirmed in your proposal.

Is it suitable for maintenance staff?

Yes. Technical English for Aviation is designed specifically for maintenance and engineering personnel.
