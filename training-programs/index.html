12 — How we deliver

Three ways to run your program.

Every program on this page can be delivered in any of these formats, and every one of them is built around your group — its size, its level and the results you need.

  1. 01

    Accelerated Certification Programs

    Short, intensive 10–15 day programs in Türkiye. Classroom sessions with university academics and practitioners, plus institutional and field visits, cultural programme and certificate ceremony.

  2. 02

    On-Site Certification Programs

    We bring the whole programme to your country. Ideal for groups of 40 or more — the same trainers, the same certificate, delivered in your own institution with no travel cost per participant.

  3. 03

    Advance Training Master Certificate Programs

    Longer, deeper programmes drawn from more than 40 specialty courses, combining several modules into a master certificate track for senior staff and specialist teams.
